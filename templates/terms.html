LIBXNDR · SHARED SHELVES

Use the library thoughtfully.

LibXNDR is a community reading tool for discoverable knowledge, personal reading memory, and responsibly shared sources.

Effective October 15, 2026

Your account

You may browse the public collection without an account. An account is required for personal shelves, progress, highlights, notes, conversations, batch finding, and private contributions. Keep your credentials secure and use the service lawfully. You are responsible for activity performed through your account.

Books, PDFs, and links

Only upload a file when you are permitted to possess and share it—for example, public-domain, openly licensed, or otherwise lawful fair-use material. If a file should not be copied, submit a public source link instead. By submitting material for library review, you confirm that your submission is lawful and permit LibXNDR to inspect, describe, and, if approved, make that source or link available in the public catalog.

Private submissions and review

A new submission enters your private shelf first. Authorized reviewers may inspect the source and submission metadata to correct the title and author, check the source, and approve or decline public inclusion. Declining a submission does not automatically remove your private shelf copy. Never upload confidential, personal, or sensitive material.

Third-party sources

LibXNDR often points to material hosted elsewhere. A LINK label means the source remains on a third-party site; LibXNDR does not control its availability, security, accuracy, or terms. A finder result is a lead, not a legal determination or endorsement. Check the source before saving, downloading, or sharing it.

Respectful use

Do not use LibXNDR to infringe rights, distribute malware, evade access controls, scrape the service abusively, harass people, impersonate others, or interfere with other readers. We may restrict accounts, decline submissions, or remove material to protect the library and its users.

Availability and removal

The service is provided as available and may change, experience errors, or lose access to third-party sources. Reading progress and annotations should not be treated as the only copy of important work. Rights holders and users may request review or removal at waleedraza1211@gmail.com; include the title, URL, and basis for the request.
