LibXNDR · Reader trust

Privacy, in plain language.

LibXNDR remembers your reading so you can return to it. It does not sell your personal information.

Effective October 15, 2026

What we collect

When you make an account, we receive your email address and the profile name or image you choose. We store the books you save, reading status and page progress, highlights, notes, and conversations you intentionally create. If you contribute a PDF or link, we store that source and the title, author, credit, and review information you provide. We also keep limited security and operational logs needed to run the service.

How it is used

We use this information to authenticate you, sync your shelf, restore reading progress, show your private reading memory, review proposed additions, prevent abuse, and improve reliability. Reading memory is private to the reader. Submissions are reviewed by Waleed alone. He can inspect a submitted source and its submission details, but never your highlights, notes, or private reading conversations.

Services that help run LibXNDR

Supabase provides authentication, database, and private-storage infrastructure. Google Cloud provides application hosting and storage for the reviewed public collection. Google may provide sign-in when you choose “Continue with Google.” These providers process only the information needed to deliver their part of the service under their own privacy terms.

Public and private material

Approved catalog records, covers, public PDFs, and outbound source links are visible to guests. Personal shelf data and unapproved uploads remain private to their owner, with submitted source material visible to Waleed for review. Approval creates a public catalog edition; it does not publish the rest of your account or reading memory.

Cookies, retention, and control

LibXNDR uses an essential session cookie to keep you signed in and short-lived security cookies during sign-in or password recovery. We retain account data while your account is active and as reasonably needed for security, moderation, and legal obligations. You may ask to access, correct, or delete your account information by contacting us.
